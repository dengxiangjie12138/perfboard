PDF embedding must use subset:false. Actual PyMuPDF pixel rendering verified the full
static TrueType font's Chinese, numbers, and electrical labels. Both OTF embedding
modes and subsetted TTF were rejected after actual pixel checks.
Export reads this bundled font and needs no installed system font or internet access.

Engineering text disables glyph substitution features (liga/kern/locl/ccmp/frac/numr/dnom).
This preserves literal identifiers and numerals in the PDF Unicode map as well as pixels.
